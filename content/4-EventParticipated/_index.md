# Technology Events & Workshops Attended

During my internship at the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program, I had the opportunity to participate in technology events and knowledge-sharing sessions organized by AWS and the AWS Study Group community. These activities helped me expand my understanding of Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence, and modern software development trends, while providing opportunities to connect and learn from industry professionals and fellow technology enthusiasts.

Below is a summary of the five events I attended during my internship:

---

### [Event 1: Buildrathon – Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global](4.1-Event1/)

- **Event Name:** Buildrathon – Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global
- **Time:** 10:30 – 12:00, Saturday, September 26, 2026
- **Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.
- **Participation Role:** Attendee
- **Key Highlights:** Kickoff of FCAJ Buildrathon 2026 Season 01; explored approaches to building and optimizing AI Agents through the talk show "The Thinking Behind Building The Bot"; connected with the AWS Study Group community and CMC Global.

---

### [Event 2: AWS Cloud & AI Day 2026 – HCMC Watch Party](4.2-Event2/)

- **Event Name:** AWS Cloud & AI Day 2026 – HCMC Watch Party
- **Time:** 08:00 – 17:00, Tuesday, September 29, 2026
- **Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.
- **Participation Role:** Attendee
- **Key Highlights:** Attended streamed sessions from AWS Cloud & AI Day in Hanoi; explored trends in Agentic AI, Data Foundation, and Cloud Modernization; connected and exchanged knowledge with the Cloud & AI community in Ho Chi Minh City.

---

### [Event 3: Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner – Navigating the Future of Cloud & AI in Vietnam](4.3-Event3/)

- **Event Name:** Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner – Navigating the Future of Cloud & AI in Vietnam
