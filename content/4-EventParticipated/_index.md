# Technology Events & Workshops Participated

During my internship in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program, I had the opportunity to attend technology events and knowledge-sharing sessions organized by AWS and the AWS Study Group community. These activities helped me broaden my understanding of Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence, and modern software development trends while providing opportunities to connect with technology professionals and fellow learners.

Below is a summary of four events I attended during my internship:

---

### [Event 1: Buildrathon – Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global](4.1-Event1/)

- **Event Name:** Buildrathon – Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global
- **Time:** 10:30 AM – 12:00 PM, Saturday, September 26, 2026
- **Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.
- **Role:** Participant
- **Key Focus:** Kickoff of FCAJ Buildrathon 2026 Season 01; exploring AI Agent design and optimization through the talk show "The Thinking Behind Building The Bot"; networking with the AWS Study Group community and CMC Global.

---

### [Event 2: AWS Cloud & AI Day 2026 – HCMC Watch Party](4.2-Event2/)

- **Event Name:** AWS Cloud & AI Day 2026 – HCMC Watch Party
- **Time:** 08:00 AM – 05:00 PM, Tuesday, September 29, 2026
- **Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.
- **Role:** Attendee
- **Key Focus:** Following presentations from AWS Cloud & AI Day in Hanoi; exploring Agentic AI, Data Foundation, and Cloud Modernization; connecting and exchanging knowledge with the Cloud & AI community in Ho Chi Minh City.

---

### [Event 3: Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner – Navigating the Future of Cloud & AI in Vietnam](4.3-Event3/)

- **Event Name:** Fireside Chat with Dr. Werner – Navigating the Future of Cloud & AI in Vietnam
- **Date:** October 2, 2026
- **Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.
- **Role:** Attendee
- **Key Focus:** Learning from Dr. Werner Vogels, CTO of Amazon, about the origins of AWS, Engineering at Scale, the evolving role of developers in the AI era, and modern software engineering practices.

---

### [Event 4: Cloud & AI Infrastructure Meetup](4.4-Event4/)

- **Event Name:** Cloud & AI Infrastructure Meetup
- **Date:** October 24, 2026
- **Location:** AWS Vietnam Office, 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam.
- **Role:** Attendee
- **Key Focus:** Participating in a community meetup focused on Cloud Computing, AI Infrastructure, and topics related to developing and operating modern technology infrastructure.
