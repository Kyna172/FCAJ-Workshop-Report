# Internship Reflections & Constructive Feedback

During my participation in the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** internship program, I have had the opportunity to explore cloud computing, learn about Amazon Web Services (AWS), attend technology events, and experience the process of working on a project with my team members.

As an Information Security student at FPT University, participating in the First Cloud AI Journey program has provided me with an opportunity to broaden my knowledge beyond my academic specialization. Through exploring AWS services and collaborating with my team on a practical project, I have gained additional perspectives on how technology systems and applications are developed, deployed, and operated in real-world environments.

Beyond technical knowledge, the program has also provided valuable experiences in workplace environments, independent learning, teamwork, and technical documentation.

---

### Evaluation of the Program

#### 1. Learning and Working Environment

I appreciate the flexible learning environment provided by the FCAJ program, where students can independently research documentation, practice at home, register for office working sessions, and participate in community activities.

Working at the office provides opportunities to interact with students from different universities, communicate directly with team members, and become familiar with a practical working environment.

Participating in events such as Buildrathon Kickoff, AWS Cloud & AI Day, FCAJ Meetup, and Cloud Mastery has also allowed me to explore various technology topics, learn from experienced professionals, and broaden my perspective on the information technology industry.

#### 2. Learning Resources and Mentorship

The learning materials and workshops provided by the AWS Study Group are valuable resources that help me gradually become familiar with AWS services and independently explore new topics.

The program emphasizes independent learning and research. This presents both an opportunity and a challenge for students with limited experience in cloud computing.

Guidance from mentors, combined with discussions among team members, has helped me identify relevant learning topics and gradually adapt to the program's requirements.

#### 3. Technical Knowledge and Practical Relevance

Although cloud computing is not the primary focus of my current academic specialization, the knowledge gained from the program remains a valuable supplement to my studies.

Exploring services such as AWS IAM, Amazon VPC, Amazon EC2, Amazon S3, Amazon CloudWatch, and AWS WAF has helped me better understand resource management, access control, system protection, and monitoring in cloud environments.

Additionally, researching AWS Budgets, Cost Explorer, and the costs associated with AI services has helped me recognize that developing a practical system requires consideration not only of technical requirements but also of operational costs and resource efficiency.

These additional perspectives and areas of knowledge may prove useful in my future studies and professional activities.

#### 4. Teamwork Experience in the ReLoop Project

Participating in the ReLoop project with the AWSS3nt1n4L team has been one of the notable experiences of my internship.

Each team member has different knowledge, strengths, and responsibilities. In my cybersecurity and documentation role, I focus on understanding the system, researching security-related topics, participating in website testing, documenting identified issues, and contributing to project documentation.

Through collaborative work, I have gained a better understanding of how different roles contribute to a technology project, including frontend development, business logic implementation, AI integration, testing, and documentation.

This experience has also helped me recognize that team members can contribute to shared outcomes through different responsibilities without necessarily participating directly in every stage of system development.

#### 5. Personal Skills Development

In addition to acquiring knowledge about AWS, the program has provided opportunities to improve my independent learning, information research, knowledge organization, and technical communication skills.

Writing four technical blog posts, maintaining weekly worklogs, documenting technology events, and preparing the Proposal and Workshop have helped me improve my ability to organize and present technical information.

Building a bilingual internship report website using Hugo, managing source files through GitHub, and deploying the website through GitHub Pages have also introduced me to online documentation management and publishing workflows.

These skills are applicable across different areas of information technology, rather than being limited to cloud computing.

### Retrospective Questions

#### What are you most proud of during your internship?

One of the most meaningful aspects of my internship is the opportunity to explore a technology field outside the primary focus of my academic specialization, gradually become familiar with AWS services, and participate in a practical team project.

Although I am not directly responsible for the entire development or deployment process of ReLoop, I have had opportunities to contribute through research, testing, issue documentation, and technical documentation.

Completing four technical blog posts and building my personal internship report website are also meaningful achievements. These deliverables reflect my efforts to research, consolidate knowledge, and complete the assigned internship activities.

#### What could the program organizers improve for future cohorts?

1. **Clarify internship report requirements**

   I would appreciate more detailed guidance regarding the expected content and scope of each report section, particularly the Proposal and Workshop for team-based projects. Clearly distinguishing shared project outcomes from individual contributions would help students prepare their documentation more effectively.

2. **Provide learning paths for different academic backgrounds**

   Students participating in the program may come from various academic disciplines, including Software Engineering, Information Security, Artificial Intelligence, and related fields. In addition to the foundational AWS learning path, the program could recommend optional learning directions or topics suited to different academic backgrounds.

3. **Strengthen AWS cost management guidance**

   For students who are new to cloud computing, managing costs and using AWS Credits effectively are important considerations. The program could provide more practical examples of service costs, resource monitoring, and precautions when practicing with AWS services.

4. **Introduce additional progress reviews and discussion sessions**

   Additional short discussions between mentors and project teams at different development stages would provide opportunities for students to present their progress, clarify difficulties, and receive feedback before completing their products and final reports.

5. **Encourage interaction between project teams**

   Knowledge-sharing sessions covering project ideas, system architectures, implementation experiences, and encountered challenges would allow students to learn from other teams participating in the program.

#### Would you recommend the program to your friends and junior students?

Yes. I would recommend the First Cloud AI Journey program to students interested in exploring cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and AWS services.

For students from other academic disciplines, the program can also provide an opportunity to broaden their knowledge, gain teamwork experience, and explore technologies used in practical environments.

However, I believe that each student should consider their learning objectives, career direction, and the program's relevance to their academic specialization to make the most of their internship experience.
