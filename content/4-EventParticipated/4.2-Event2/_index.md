### Event Objectives

- Explore emerging trends in Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence.
- Learn about Agentic AI, data infrastructure, and cloud modernization.
- Gain insights into how businesses use AWS services to develop and operate their systems.
- Connect and exchange knowledge with members of the AWS technology community in Vietnam.

### Key Highlights

#### Agentic AI & Modern Software Development

- Explored the growing role of AI Agents in supporting tasks within software applications.
- Gained insights into the considerations involved in moving AI Agents from experimentation to real-world operations.
- Developed a broader understanding of how AI contributes to software development and product improvement.

#### Data Foundation & Cloud Modernization

- Learned about the importance of data infrastructure in developing Cloud and AI applications.
- Explored general approaches to modernizing systems and applications in cloud environments.
- Recognized the importance of selecting architectures that align with real-world business requirements.

#### Cloud Operations & Cost Optimization

- Explored important considerations when deploying and operating applications on AWS.
- Gained insights into balancing performance, cost, and scalability in cloud environments.
- Learned about discussions concerning cloud cost optimization and practical AI adoption.

### Key Takeaways

- **Cloud & AI Trends:** Gained a broader understanding of how Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence are increasingly integrated into software development.
- **Agentic AI:** Developed an initial understanding of the challenges involved in moving AI solutions from prototypes to real-world applications.
- **System Architecture:** Recognized the importance of selecting architectures that match system requirements and scale.
- **Cloud Cost Optimization:** Became more aware of the importance of monitoring and optimizing cloud service costs.
- **Continuous Learning:** Recognized the need to continuously update technical knowledge and stay informed about emerging technologies.

### Application to Work

- **AWS Architecture Exploration:** Continued researching AWS services and how they can be integrated into application architectures.
- **ReLoop Project Development:** Considered architecture selection principles, performance, and cost when participating in discussions about the team project.
- **Cloud & AI Knowledge Development:** Continued exploring topics introduced during the event throughout the internship.

Participating in **AWS Cloud & AI Day 2026 – HCMC Watch Party** at the AWS Vietnam office provided an opportunity to follow presentations from the main event in Hanoi and gain additional perspectives on emerging trends in Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence.

#### Attending the Watch Party

- Followed presentations broadcast from AWS Cloud & AI Day in Hanoi.

#### Networking and Discussions

- The event provided opportunities for participants to exchange knowledge and perspectives on technology-related topics.

- Developed a better understanding of the value of knowledge sharing within the AWS Study Group community.
