## Event Recap: [View on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/share/p/1E3kZ8yC7h/)

### Event Objectives

* Share cloud computing knowledge and learning experiences with students.
* Address questions related to technical knowledge, essential skills, and career development in cloud computing.
* Provide opportunities for students with a shared passion for technology to connect and learn from one another.

### Key Highlights

#### Cloud Knowledge Sharing & Career Guidance

* **Learning Roadmap**: Discussed approaches to learning cloud computing and the essential skills students should develop.
* **Career Guidance**: Explored potential career paths in cloud computing through practical questions raised by attendees.

#### Question and Answer (Q&A) Session

* **Technical Knowledge Exchange**: Addressed students' questions regarding cloud learning and technical skill development.
* **Experience Sharing**: Explored different perspectives on the challenges of learning cloud computing and personal development.
* The Q&A session was highly interactive and lasted longer than expected due to active student participation.

#### End-of-Event Quiz Activity

* **Knowledge Review**: An interactive quiz helped attendees reinforce their knowledge while creating an engaging atmosphere at the end of the session.
* The activity received enthusiastic participation from more than 100 participants.

In addition, the organizers introduced AWS Student Community Day Vietnam 2026, which was scheduled to take place the following weekend.

### Lessons Learned

#### Knowledge & Career Development

* **Building Cloud Foundations**: Recognized the importance of combining foundational cloud knowledge with practical experience.
* **Personal Development**: Gained additional perspectives on learning roadmaps and the skills required to develop a career in cloud computing.

#### Learning Skills & Community Networking

* **Proactive Learning**: Understood the value of asking questions, exchanging knowledge, and learning from experienced professionals.
* **Community Networking**: Recognized the importance of community activities in expanding knowledge and learning opportunities.

### Application to Work

* **Reinforcing Technical Knowledge**: Continue reviewing cloud computing knowledge and combining it with information security skills throughout the internship.
* **Learning Direction**: Review accumulated knowledge and identify skills that require further improvement after the internship.
* **Proactive Networking & Learning**: Actively participate in AWS community activities to exchange knowledge and practical experiences.

### Event Experience

The **Cloud Mastery event (November 14, 2026)** at the AWS Vietnam office, Bitexco Financial Tower, provided an open environment for students interested in cloud computing to exchange knowledge and experiences.

#### Networking & Knowledge Exchange

* Impressed by the proactive participation of students during the Q&A session, particularly their questions about technical knowledge and career development.
* Had the opportunity to explore different perspectives on learning and professional development in cloud computing.

#### Personal Development & Career Direction

* Reflected on the knowledge and experiences accumulated during the internship at First Cloud AI Journey.
* Gained additional motivation to continue developing cloud knowledge and improving technical skills in information security.

> Overall, Cloud Mastery was not only a knowledge-sharing session but also an opportunity for students to connect, exchange experiences, and develop a clearer direction for their future personal and professional growth.
