### Event Objectives

- Launch the FCAJ Buildrathon 2026 Season 01, providing participants with opportunities to take part in challenges focused on building Cloud and AI-powered solutions.
- Introduce the direction, activities, and challenges of the Buildrathon program.
- Share approaches to designing and optimizing AI Agents through the talk show "The Thinking Behind Building The Bot".
- Provide opportunities to interact, learn, and connect with technology professionals from AWS Study Group and CMC Global.
- Offer insights into career opportunities and professional development paths in Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence.

### Speakers and Participating Organizations

- **AWS Study Group:** The community organizing the program and connecting members interested in AWS Cloud and AI.
- **CMC Global:** A supporting organization sharing practical knowledge about AI Agents, technical challenges, and career opportunities.
- **Technology Professionals:** Experts sharing their experiences in developing AI solutions and addressing common challenges in enterprise environments.

#### FCAJ Buildrathon 2026 Season 01 Kickoff

- Understanding the program's objectives in encouraging participants to develop Cloud and AI-powered products.
- Exploring the importance of hands-on learning, problem-solving, and real-world product development.
- Connecting with a community of more than 300 participants at the AWS Vietnam office.

#### The Thinking Behind Building The Bot

- Exploring the fundamental approaches to designing and developing AI Agents.
- Recognizing the importance of defining objectives and selecting appropriate architectures before developing a Bot.
- Learning about key considerations when building and optimizing AI Agents for enterprise environments.
- Understanding that an effective AI solution depends not only on the AI model but also on suitable system architecture and processing workflows.

#### AI Agent Challenge

- Learning about the technical challenges introduced during the program.
- Exploring approaches to analyzing requirements and identifying solutions to real-world problems.
- Recognizing the importance of testing, evaluation, and continuous improvement in AI Agent development.
- Understanding the value of combining Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence when building software products.

#### Career Opportunities with CMC Global

- Exploring career opportunities and professional development paths in Cloud Computing and AI.
- Gaining insights into the skills required to participate in enterprise technology projects.
- Recognizing the importance of practical experience, self-directed learning, and teamwork.
- Having opportunities to connect and interact with recruitment representatives from CMC Global during the event.

### Event Experience

Participating in **FCAJ Buildrathon 2026 Season 01 – Kickoff: Code the Future with CMC Global** at the AWS Vietnam office provided a valuable opportunity to gain practical knowledge and perspectives on Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence.

#### Buildrathon Kickoff Atmosphere

- The event brought together more than 300 participants, creating an engaging environment for networking and learning.
- Had opportunities to meet individuals who shared an interest in AWS technologies and AI.
- Gained a better understanding of the AWS Study Group's "Pay It Forward" spirit, emphasizing learning, knowledge sharing, and community support.

#### Exploring AI Agent Development Approaches

- The talk show "The Thinking Behind Building The Bot" provided additional perspectives on the process of developing AI solutions.
